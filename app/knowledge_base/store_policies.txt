STORE POLICY KNOWLEDGE BASE

DELIVERY

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 3 to 6 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days in major cities.

Q: When is my order packed?
A: Orders are usually packed within 24 hours after confirmation.

Q: Will I receive a tracking ID?
A: Yes. A tracking ID is generated for every confirmed order so customers can track their shipment.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Customers can track their order using the tracking ID provided after the order is shipped.

Q: Why is my order delayed?
A: Delivery delays may occur due to high demand, logistics issues, or weather conditions.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Yes. Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

Q: Can I exchange a product?
A: Yes. Exchanges can be requested within 7 days of delivery.

Q: What are the return conditions?
A: The product must be unused, unwashed, and have the original tags attached.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product passes the quality check.

PAYMENTS

Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: The store supports UPI, credit card, debit card, net banking, and Cash on Delivery for eligible pin codes.

Q: Can I pay with Cash on Delivery?
A: Yes. Cash on Delivery is available for certain pin codes depending on delivery availability.

SUPPORT AND TICKETS

Q: When will a support ticket be created?
A: A support ticket is created when the chatbot cannot resolve the issue or when customers report problems such as:
- damaged product
- wrong product
- delivery failure
- refund issues

Q: How will my issue be resolved after ticket creation?
A: The support team reviews the ticket and updates the status until the issue is resolved.
